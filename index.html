05 / About

From the classroom to national strategy.

Hany Moussa leads national programs across performance management, educator competency standards, AI enablement, and talent development at the UAE Ministry of Education.

His work spans the redesign of a ministry performance platform for ~25,000 users, the UAE National Educators’ Competency Framework (2025), and a competency-based skill-gap assessment for 13,000+ teachers and leaders. He has served as senior project manager and technical lead for AI-in-education initiatives with the UAE Office of Artificial Intelligence and OpenAI–G42.

Earlier, as an AI developer and data analyst, he built MurshidAI and a Power BI platform integrating data from 240,000+ students. A decade of mathematics leadership preceded his move into strategy, including a 23% improvement in results driven by data-led intervention. He blends program leadership, policy alignment, analytics, and digital product delivery.
